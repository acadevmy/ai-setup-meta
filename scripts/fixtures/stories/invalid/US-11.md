**User Story**
As a customer, I want to edit and delete my address, so that my deliveries reach me.

**Acceptance Criteria**
**Scenarios**

**Scenario:** two actions
**Given** a saved address
**When** the customer edits it
**When** the customer deletes it
**Then** the list is updated

**Scenario:** a long scenario with no loading cycle
**Given** a saved address
**And** a second saved address
**When** the customer opens their addresses
**Then** they see the first address
**And** they see the second address
**And** they see how many addresses they have
